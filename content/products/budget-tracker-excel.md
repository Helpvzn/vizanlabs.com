**What this template is and why it was built:**

This is a professionally researched, Dubai-specific CV template that solves two problems at once — passing through ATS (Applicant Tracking System) software, and impressing the human recruiter who reads it after. Dubai's job market is one of the most competitive in the world, with thousands of applications arriving daily for a single role. This template was designed with that reality in mind.

***

**Design Philosophy:**

The color scheme uses **Deep Navy (#1A2744) and Premium Gold (#B8972E)** — the visual language of Dubai's luxury, finance, real estate, and corporate sectors. Multinational companies and top UAE employers respond to this aesthetic. Font choices are **Calibri (Word)** and **Helvetica (PDF)** — both are ATS-safe, look clean on screen, and print sharply. Decorative or creative fonts regularly fail ATS parsing.

***

**Dubai-Specific Features built into this template:**

Visa status and nationality in the header is standard UAE practice — Western CVs skip this, but Dubai recruiters look for it immediately to assess sponsorship requirements and availability. The phone number is formatted with +971. The certifications section highlights **RERA, PMP, and Salesforce** — credentials that carry direct weight in the UAE market. The achievements section uses **AED currency** because Gulf hiring managers assess numbers in local currency — it reads as local market experience, not foreign.

***

**ATS Compliance:**

Single-column layout is used because multi-column designs cause ATS to read content in the wrong order, scrambling your information. No text boxes, images, infographics, or critical details buried in headers or footers. Bullets are in standard format. Section headings are plain text. The PDF is text-based, not scanned, so ATS can fully parse it. This means the template works across **Bayt, Naukrigulf, LinkedIn UAE, and direct company career portals** without formatting failures.

***

**Who this template is best for:**

Business Development, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Real Estate, Consulting, IT Management — any mid-to-senior level professional targeting Dubai or the broader GCC region (Abu Dhabi, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman). Freshers can adapt it by replacing the experience section with internships, academic projects, and transferable skills.
